Under-load latency inflation = interactive requests stuck behind bulk on the
ONE shared tunnel connection (queueing/bufferbloat without loss; head-of-line
blocking with loss). QUIC's per-stream delivery is what fixes this.

  clean   4KiB   p95      42ms ->      415ms under load  (x9.8)  <== inflated
  clean   65KiB  p95      45ms ->      531ms under load  (x11.9)  <== inflated

  bursty  65KiB  p95     106ms ->     3530ms under load  (x33.3)  <== inflated

VERDICT: shared-connection HEAD-OF-LINE / bufferbloat blocking IS present.
  Interactive latency inflates up to ~38x under concurrent bulk load
  (worse, and appearing even without loss). For a busy multi-user app this is a
  real UX risk, and QUIC (independent streams) is the direct fix — leans TOWARD Part B.
